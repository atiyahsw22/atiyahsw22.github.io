i guess i'll always be a dreamer: a short story of loneliness

i heard him, i didn't just want to dance.



[dreamer, atiyah saleemah, 2026]

there's something about a family florida trip that always seems to stick a butter knife deeper into a wound i haven't fully grown a scab on. with disneyworld at a short distance and geckos by the pool, the sound of laughter around from my relatives pushes me further into a tomorrowland. meaning, i would rather be anywhere but there.

it sounds harsh but that's why you'll keep reading, so i can explain.

★

it goes without question that i love michael jackson and anything jackson-related. i’ve been a fan of his and the family since i was seven years old. it is actually michael jackson who sparked my maladaptive daydreaming and my vast imagination. i can remember being a little girl in the backseat of my mom’s car, staring out the window into the night's sky as i listened to “maybe tomorrow” and imagined myself dating little michael; he was my first real celebrity crush outside of arnez from one on one.

most kids like me [at that time] have gone through the inevitable m.j. phase...they might not have gone as far as to date him in their head, but you get my jest. generations of children continue to go through this phase, eons after michael’s prime years and well into his unfortunate death. the new biopic starring his nephew jaafar has since elongated his legacy to precede any of us, with the constant saying that “michael jackson has fans not even born yet,” deemed to be true each day.

the personal lens of the film created scenes that felt far more soul-reaching than the score had. we as fans connected less with michael the machine and more with michael the man. i felt so deeply touched by the movie that i sat in the theatre long after the ending credits, simply blown away by not what i just saw but what i instantly felt; relief.

there’s a scene... you know, the viral one that everybody's talking about; the one where mike just finished cleaning the toy store out, he comes home, and he asks his big brothers[who are already shooting hoops together] to play twister with him. then they all proceed to tell him every other excuse in the world as to why they can't.

so what does mike do, you ask? michael goes and plays the game anyway, but he's not alone. he plays a game of twister with his baby chimp, bubbles.

it was in that moment in the dark of the theatre that twenty-something years of feelings, i’ve never been able to accurately describe nor transparently express, came rushing through my veins like i stuck a needle in them jawns.

it was michael[and bubbles] who showed me what my loneliness truly looked like.

for the first time in my life, the indescribable loneliness that i’ve always felt had been personified in the most poetic way; loneliness isn’t always being in sole isolation; true loneliness is the feeling of being surrounded by so many people and yet feeling connected to no one.

that was the recurring theme throughout the movie, if you ask me. outside of mike finding solace in his music, he found it within his animals instead of fellow humans and comrades.

i wish i could blame him, but i can't because i know how that feels. i feel like that when all of my cousins subconsiously makes florida the bane of existence. last year, they all went on a cousins trip there without inviting me, a few years prior during the family trip; they all went to the club without inviting me as i sat in my hotel room[i chalk that one up to i had an injured knee], and then years before that they did something similar in the same sunshine state, but we were all teens back then, so maybe they just went joyriding.

out of sympathy, i got invited to the next hangout where i felt everything inluding ostracized. but i was surrounded by blinking lights and go karts, so i shoudl've been fine right? hell no lol

that moment always stuck with me because i didn't quite understand it. i didn't quite understand what i was doing for them to not really wanna kick it with me like that? i know i'm quiet but damn, i atleast deserved and still deserve an olive branch. they just treat me like i'm the cousin they didn't grow up with. i could see if i was the cousin that only came around for funerals but it isn't like that at all and that's why even when i'm around them, i still don't feel like i'm all the way there or in tune with them.

this feeling has followed me for a long time though, so i don't want to place any blame solely on them. i can remember feeling lonely when i was looking out of windows and imagining myself escaping to other worlds, where people looked at me with understanding eyes and patient smiles. it felt like a distant dream that i could only be a part of when i closed my eyes at night.

i wrote so many versions of this article because i was afraid of offending the same people who wouldn’t dare call me on a random tuesday to wonder if i ate or even woke up that day. keeping up with me on social media is the only way they know what i look like now.

i can’t tell you the last time many people i know checked on my wellbeing, yet i’m the one they come to for all the answers to their problems. this is my reality, but that dream world i talked about earlier? there’s a constant reciprocal energy resting in the belly of a pete rock beat loop.

i can only count on one hand the figures in my life that try to reach inside my heart and hug it with their warm essence. where is everyone else? i don’t know, and i so desperately wish i could tell you.

i know what you’re thinking: why is she so worried about people that don’t do for her yet barely giving light to the people that do? well, my only answer to that would be because the people that don’t do for me are the main ones that always want something from me; my time, my wisdom, and my undying loyalty. but i can’t even get a happy birthday unless i’ve posted it on a 24-hour story.

maybe this article has become less about loneliness and more about how i’ve failed to create boundaries with people and to put on notice that my meter to please everyone has run out. it should be about that time in my life where my exhaustion is turning into pure rage over what i’ve allowed others to take from me without receiving anything in return.

in the morning, nobody is hitting my line besides shein and ulta beauty. my disconnection to others has a gap that falls even wider as i conquer my day with a smile and some laughter from the pits of my stomach.

there's parts of me that have just gotten used to it, so i'm numb, but i'm content to a certain degree. meaning, i make do with what i have in life. i still try to have a good time, even if it's all in my head where scenarios place me at the center of everyone’s attention and love. in these fantasies, i dance just like michael, but in the real world, i face his same blues.

only difference between me and michael is that i don’t have a monkey to play twister with me, i just have the four walls of my room.
