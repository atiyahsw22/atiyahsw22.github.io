pearl and her delusional rage to be somebody

in her case, it's get rich or kill trying.



[rage iz queen, atiyah saleemah, 2026]

before we get started, i just want to say that killing someone is totally not the answer. please do not do that! also, no, i have never, nor have i ever thought about killing anyone.

okay, now that we got that out the way, can we really talk? like for real?

the movie pearl that was released in 2022, starring mia goth, followed a sinister path right into the life of a young lady named pearl. according to wikipedia, "the film serves as an origin story for the titular villain, whose fervent aspiration to become a movie star clashes with her home life and violent tendencies, leading to violent acts on her family's texas homestead in 1918."

before watching pearl, i assumed it was just another slasher film where the protagonist has no real meaning or depth to their character outside hurting people. i thought i would watch an hour and some change of a little girl running around a farm just cutting chickens' heads off for the hell of it. but damn it, i'd be lying if i said i didn't end the movie feeling a sense of sadness as well as some sort of demented reverence for her. my mind started to fill with queries: do i understand her? do i relate to her? in some ways, am i her?

the answer is yes. yes, i am. in some ways, i am pearl. in some ways of my life, i do have a deep motivating rage inside of me just like she does. because deep down, all the gore and blood gave me a clearer understanding as to what pearl wasn't mainly about.

pearl represents more than a psychotic maniac to me; she represents a large part of me that so desperately wants to get off the freaking farm! in my case, my farm isn't acres of land; it's the confinements of my reality, the place i’m currently in, the four walls of my room, and the uncertainty that comes with not having a lot of money.

pearl represents a large part of us that just wants to get the hell up out of here and do more meaningful things with our lives.

'cause peep, pearl during the whole movie just wanted to be somebody. she just wanted an equal shot at fame, love, and admiration. don't we all?!

let's not pretend like when we saw her sob because she didn't get the audition, we didn't think back to those rejecting moments of our lives where, yet again, our dreams felt so far away. her desire to be desired was tarnished in that instant, and whatever hope she had left to be a star? well, she transformed that into ire, which is why we saw her do those sick things to others. because what if i say they were just collateral damage in the grand scheme of her life?

it's an extremely hard struggle to have optimistic dreams, while simultaneously operating with a small sense of fear that maybe they will never come true. that nightly knees-on-the-ground prayer is held together by a mustard seed, and everything else is grit and tenacity born out of survival.

mitsy represents the girl she wants to be like; projectionist represents the man she wants to love her, and her parents represent the resentment she has towards them and the world for how she's ended up in her current spot. they were all obstacles she faced that all of us sane folks learn to jump over or let crumble us. i guess you could say she did a little bit of both. in this case, she allowed her anger to overpower her emotions so badly that it crumbled her spirit, and she subconsciously sabotaged her destiny even more. it is in those moments, where all hope is lost, that people will continue to burn more bridges than they’ve built because they have nothing else to lose.

i don’t want to be like that. i don’t want to let my reality, because it hasn't turned out how i imagined it to be yet, harden my heart and make me susceptible to accepting that i’ll put my own self in danger in an attempt to save myself.

i feel like if i do that, then i'll be everything that every naysayer and hater wants me to be; nothing. and i refuse to let that happen; i refuse to have that be my story. but that's also what makes life so hard, guys.

because what will i ever do if i have to waste yet another year of my life on this very farm i never wanted to raise cows and chickens on in the first place?
